Sessions

A session is your unit of work: one isolated sandbox, one terminal, one 24-hour clock. The header pill always shows the remaining time — amber under an hour, red under fifteen minutes.

24-hour lifecycle

Every session expires 24 hours after creation. Expiry is enforced on every request: the sandbox is stopped and all session data — history, shares, env vars — is deleted. Expired sessions return 410 Gone, never stale output.

Compute renewal

Cloud sandboxes on this plan live at most ~40 minutes each. When a sandbox ends while your session is still alive, Console transparently provisions a fresh one on your next action and logs a sandbox renewed event. Your history and share links survive; the sandbox filesystem starts fresh, and exposed ports get new URLs.

History

Every session records commands, output, exit codes, resizes and signals. Reopening a session — or opening a share link — replays the transcript, so context is never lost when compute renews.

Ending a session

Press ⏻ Terminate in the session bar to stop the sandbox and delete everything immediately.
